# 05.1 — Deal Decision

## Intent

The pure decision that turns a match into an *alertable deal* (or not). A match means "this
watched thing is in a flyer"; a deal means "and it's actually worth telling you about". This
is where `maxPrice`, `requireSale`, `minDiscountPct` (04.1) and the price-history verdict
(07.3) combine. Getting this right is the difference between a useful alert stream and one
you mute in a week.

## Decision inputs and rule

```scala
def decide(m: Match, watch: WatchItem, verdict: DealVerdict): AlertDecision
```

Where `DealVerdict` comes from 07.3 (how this price compares to history). Rule (pinned):

1. If the watch sets `maxPrice` and the observation has an effective price **above** it → no
   alert. (An observation with *no* price passes this gate only if `maxPrice` is unset.)
2. If `requireSale` is true, the verdict must be at least "notable" (07.3) — a price at or
   above its own history is not a sale even if it's under `maxPrice`.
3. If `minDiscountPct` is set, the discount depth (from `original_price` or history baseline)
   must meet it.
4. If all set conditions pass → `Alert(deal, score)`; else `Suppress(reason)`.

A `Suppress` carries a reason (for 08.3 diagnostics: "above max price", "not a sale", etc.).

## Gherkin

```gherkin
Feature: Decide whether a match is an alertable deal

  @pure
  Scenario: A price at or below max price with a good verdict alerts
    Given a watch with max price 3.00 and requireSale true
    And a matched observation priced 2.50 with a "good deal" verdict
    When I decide
    Then it is an alert

  @pure
  Scenario: A price above max price is suppressed
    Given a watch with max price 3.00
    And a matched observation priced 3.49
    When I decide
    Then it is suppressed with reason "above max price"

  @pure
  Scenario: requireSale suppresses a normal-priced match
    Given a watch with requireSale true and no max price
    And a matched observation whose verdict is "at or above history"
    When I decide
    Then it is suppressed with reason "not a sale"

  @pure
  Scenario: A no-price promo passes only when no max price is set
    Given a matched observation with no effective price (PercentOffUnknown)
    And a watch with no max price and requireSale false
    When I decide
    Then it is an alert
    And when the watch sets a max price
    Then it is suppressed with reason "price unknown, max price required"

  @pure
  Scenario: minDiscountPct gates on discount depth
    Given a watch requiring at least 20% off
    And a matched observation discounted 10% from its baseline
    When I decide
    Then it is suppressed with reason "discount below threshold"
