# 07.2 — Rolling Price Stats

## Intent

Compute the statistics that make a verdict possible: for a product key, the trailing
distribution of effective prices — median, min, and a recent-window comparison — weighted by
observation confidence (02.6) and provenance (07.1). Pure functions over a list of
observations; no IO. This is the quantitative heart of "is this cheap?".

## Contract

```scala
final case class PriceStats(
  key: ProductKey,
  window: Duration,
  n: Int,
  weightedMedian: Option[Money],
  min: Option[Money],
  max: Option[Money],
  lastSeen: Option[Money]
)

def rollingStats(obs: List[PriceObservation], window: Duration, now: Instant): PriceStats
```

- Only observations with an effective price contribute to numeric stats; price-absent promos
  are counted in `n` context but not in the median.
- **Weighting**: a `ScalarPrice`/`High` observation counts full; `MultiBuyUnit`/`Medium` and
  `Hammer`-fuzzy count less; `ParsedFromText`/`Low` counts least. Weighting is documented and
  tested (it's the thing a mutation would silently corrupt).
- Weighted median over a small, weighted sample is defined precisely (interpolation rule
  pinned) so results are deterministic.

## Gherkin

```gherkin

  @pure
  Scenario: Median and min are computed over priced observations only
    Given observations priced [2.50, 2.99, 3.49] and one price-absent promo
    When I compute rolling stats over a wide window
    Then the min is 2.50
    And the promo does not affect the median

  @pure
  Scenario: Higher-confidence observations weigh more in the median
    Given three observations at 2.00 (Low), 3.00 (High), 3.00 (High)

  @pure
  Scenario: Only observations within the window are included
    Given observations spanning 12 weeks
    When I compute stats over an 8-week window ending now
    Then only observations within the last 8 weeks contribute

  Scenario: An empty or all-price-absent history yields no numeric stats
    Given only price-absent promo observations
    When I compute stats
    Then the median, min, and max are all absent
    And n reflects the promo count

  @pure @property
  Scenario: min <= weighted median <= max whenever stats exist
    Given any non-empty set of priced observations
    When I compute stats
    Then min <= weighted median <= max
